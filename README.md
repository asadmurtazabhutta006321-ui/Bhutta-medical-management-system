# Bhutta Medical Management System

A comprehensive Pharmacy Management System designed to manage inventory, sales tracking, stock alerts, expiry monitoring, employee payroll, and financial profit analytics.

## Features
- **Inventory Management:** Custom medicine entry with stock & expiry tracking.
- **Alert System:** Dedicated sections for low-stock warnings and expiring medicines.
- **Employee & Payroll:** Staff management, salary structures, and payouts.
- **Sales & POS:** Daily checkout, total valuation, and automated profit/loss calculation.
